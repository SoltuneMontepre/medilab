# Laboratory questions

- **Outsourcing decision.** Who accepts the scheduler's outsourcing suggestion and creates and sends the subcontract dispatch, and who enters the results a subcontractor sends back? (blocks US-HD05)
- **Disposal.** Who disposes of retained samples and records it?
- **Report delivery.** Who delivers an issued report to the customer and records the delivery?
- **Result content.** Which machine measured a result, and who measured it and when.
