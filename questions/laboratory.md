# Laboratory questions

- **Public-holiday source.** Where does the company calendar obtain its public-holiday list: is it maintained by the administrator or imported from a source? (blocks US-HD05)
- **Default due date on a non-working day.** The default due date is worked out from the safety margin, but a due date falls on a working day. When the default lands on a weekend or a public holiday, does it move to the working day before? (blocks US-HD05)
- **Who maintains customers.** A customer's details are an Odoo contact, and Odoo lets only its contact managers create and edit contacts, so for now only administrators create and edit customers. Should sales get Odoo's contact creation group, or should a customer write its contact with full rights once the customer permission is checked, as people do? (blocks US-S03)
- **Cancelling during testing.** A test request can be cancelled while testing, but the lifecycles let only expected, received and testing samples, and waiting, assigned and testing sample tests, be cancelled. What happens to a sample on hold or awaiting approval, and to a test outsourced or with a result entered, when its request is cancelled? For now they keep their status. (US-SD06, US-S17)
- **Every test cancelled.** When the head of department cancels every sample test of a sample, nothing is left to test or report. Is the sample cancelled too, and its test request once all its samples are? For now the sample keeps its status. (US-HD09)
- **Sanity check thresholds.** A numeric result is flagged when at least 10 approved results exist and it lies more than 3 standard deviations from their mean, or within 10% of an applicable numeric limit in a requested comparison criterion. Are these the right defaults, and do some parameters need their own?
- **Signing a file version.** Is a signed file version also digitally signed through Viettel Sign, as printed documents are, and may the person who saved a version sign it? For now versions are not signed through Viettel Sign, and the person who saved a version may sign it; both are provisional, not decided. Proposed answer, pending the stakeholders' confirmation, not decided: versions are not signed through Viettel Sign, and the person who saved a version may not sign it, as for test results (#228). (blocks US-HD16)
- **Creating sample folders.** The old module let only its sync manager create a sample's folders, from the sample. Does the head of department create them, or are they created when the sample is received? The [overview](../docs/functional/laboratory_module/overview.md#laboratory-files) already says the head of department creates them from the sample; the answer confirms or corrects it. For now the head of a department that tests the sample creates them from the sample; this is provisional, not decided. Proposed answer, pending the stakeholders' confirmation, not decided: the head of department creates a sample's folders from the sample, as now (#228). (blocks US-HD15)
- **Retention of laboratory files.** When an archived folder's retention date passes, are its files deleted from the bucket, and who is told first? Versions never change and are never deleted, and content is stored once for every version that has it, also in other folders: is content another folder still keeps left in place, are signed versions and their content kept longer, and does a version whose content is removed stay in the history? Until this is answered, nothing removes stored content. Proposed answer, pending the stakeholders' confirmation, not decided: nothing is deleted automatically; once the retention date has passed, the files are flagged for an administrator, who approves their deletion; signed versions are never deleted automatically, and shared content only once no version refers to it (#226). (blocks US-AD30)
- **Medilab Sync installations in use.** The laboratory files API moves to `/medilab/api/v1`, which main does not serve under the old path `/api/sync/v1`, and the keys of installed computers were issued by the old server. Which installations of Medilab Sync are in use, against which server and release? Must they keep syncing until a cut-over, or may they be updated or reinstalled and signed in again then? Are the old server's folders and versions moved to the new one, so that the computers' bindings still find their folders? See also the question on the sync app's updates in [infrastructure questions](infrastructure.md). (blocks US-T23)
- **Restoring a revoked computer.** Revoking a computer is final, also when a person revokes their own, and a revoked installation cannot sign in again. Can an administrator restore a revoked computer? For now it gets back in only as a new installation of Medilab Sync, which signs in with a new installation id. (US-AD29)
- **Folder retention period.** Is 1825 days the required default retention period for archived laboratory folders? This remains an assumption pending the owner's confirmation. (blocks US-AD30)
- **Folder settings.** Should a newly bound folder default to upload-only? This remains an assumption pending the owner's confirmation. (blocks US-T23)
- **Deleting ways of testing.** The Ways of Testing list on the parameter form only archives a pair. The documents still let a pair that nothing refers to be deleted, and the Delete ways of testing permissions are still shipped, but no screen offers deleting one, so only the API can. Should a screen offer deleting an unused pair, or should the delete action and its permissions be removed? (US-AD06)
- **Contract management.** The documents only keep a contract reference on a subcontractor. Does the laboratory need to manage contracts, with customers, with subcontractors or both, and if so, what does a contract hold (number, dates, value, file, covered parameters), does it expire or renew, and does it limit which requests or outsourced tests can be made under it? (US-AD12)
- **Quality registration expiry.** A dossier counts as expired after its last valid day. Against which date: the sample's receipt, the result's approval or the report's issue, and which applies to a new version of an issued report? (US-AD08)
- **Samples without the customer present.** The handover record is signed by the customer. How is a sample received by courier or post, with no one to sign, recorded?
- **Anonymity beyond the label.** Testers never see the customer on a sample's label or photos, but free-text notes and the packaging can still name the customer. Are notes hidden from testers, or checked when they are written?
- **Returning samples.** A sample ends retained or disposed of, but some customers ask for what is left of their sample. Can a remaining or retained sample be returned to the customer, and how is it recorded?
- **Unsynced edits of a removed member.** A member removed from a folder keeps the files waiting on their computer, paused for good, and nobody else sees them. Are they handed over, uploaded or discarded? (US-T28)
- **A shared computer.** Medilab Sync signs in one person per installation. When another person signs in on the same computer, what happens to the first person's files still waiting to sync? (US-T23)
- **Conflicts on result files.** When a result file changed both on a computer and on the server, keeping the local file does not say which version is the result, and a version can be signed while the conflict is open. Which version counts, and is signing refused until the conflict is resolved? (US-T25, US-HD16)

## #173

- **The department of a file version.** A sample is tested by several departments, and any of them should count for a level whose signer must belong to the department that did the work. The approval chain takes one department per document, so such a level is refused for file versions for now. Should the chain take several departments, or does one of the sample's departments count? Proposed answer, pending the stakeholders' confirmation, not decided: a signer qualifies when they belong to any department that tests the sample (#228). (US-HD16)
- **When a file version's process is completed.** Once a document's process is completed, its signatures can no longer be withdrawn. When is that for a file version, for example once the test report that relies on it is issued? For now its signatures can always be withdrawn. Proposed answer, pending the stakeholders' confirmation, not decided: once the sample's test report is issued, the signatures of its versions can no longer be withdrawn (#228). (US-HD16)
- **General working and report folders.** Creating a sample's folders adds its raw-data and result folders. Who creates additional general-purpose working-documents and reports folders, and when? This remains outside the current scope. Controlled report drafts and issued PDFs are covered by [Test reports](../docs/specs/test-reports.md); their creation is part of that workflow. (US-HD15, US-AD36, US-LH02)
- **Uploads to a closed sample's folders.** The old module refused uploads to the folders of a completed or cancelled sample. Should the folders of a closed sample refuse uploads, and online editing? For now they do not; this is provisional, not decided. Proposed answer, pending the stakeholders' confirmation, not decided: the folders of a completed or cancelled sample behave like turned-off and archived folders: they are read, and every write is refused, uploads, online editing and locking included (#229).
- **Names of sample folders.** Assumption: a sample's folders are named after its lab code and their category, such as `PTN.2026.0001 Raw Data`, with the roots `PTN.2026.0001-raw` and `PTN.2026.0001-result`. Is that the name the laboratory wants?

## #171 Online editing

- **Online editing of a closed sample's files.** Asked with [uploads to a closed sample's folders](#173): whether a closed sample's folders refuse online editing too. For now they do not; this is provisional, not decided. The proposed answer is in that question (#229).

## Laboratory files API

- **An upload refused once its content is verified.** Between finalizing an upload and storing its version, something can refuse the version: someone else locks the file, the folder is turned off or archived, the person is removed from the folder or loses the laboratory files permission, or the computer is revoked. What state does the upload get? Options: (a) `failed` with the code of the refusal, its content failed and its object deleted, so the computer uploads the file again; (b) a state that does not end, with the code of the refusal, that the computer finalizes again before the upload expires, keeping its verified content; (c) stored as a conflict version, which a lock otherwise refuses; (d) ended with the code, keeping its verified content that no version refers to. For now it is (b): the upload is `refused` with the code, finalizing it again stores it once what refused it is gone, and it expires like an unfinished upload; reconciliation reports verified content no version refers to and never deletes it, as the retention of laboratory files is open. This is provisional, not decided. (US-T23)

## Catalog

- **Typed codes and sequences.** Does a code sequence skip numbers already taken by typed codes? Proposed answer, not decided: the sequence takes the next free number, and a typed code that matches the sequence's format is warned about.
- **Subcontractor contacts and companies with two roles.** What happens when a subcontractor's default contact is archived, or the company is also a customer? Proposed answer, not decided: archiving the default contact warns, and another is chosen before samples are sent; a company may be both, but never tests its own samples as a subcontractor.
- **Quality registration warnings.** Does a renewed dossier warn again, and is a missed warning sent late? Proposed answer, not decided: a dossier without an expiry date never warns; a renewal warns again from the new date; the warning goes out once N days or fewer remain and that date has not been warned about, so a missed run catches up; the lab head and administrators receive it.
- **Removing a sample type from a parameter in use.** What happens to open orders, order templates and packages? Proposed answer, not decided: the pair is no longer offered; orders already placed keep it; an order template reused later drops that line and says so; packages are still sold for other sample types.

## Sampling and collection

- **Sampling requirements in practice.** Is a way of testing offered when it has no sampling requirement for the sample type, which amount wins between the sample type's minimum and the methods' total, and how are amounts in different units, or too much for one container, handled? Proposed answer, not decided: a way of testing without a sampling requirement is not offered; expected samples use the methods' amounts, with the sample type's minimum as the floor when a method states none; amounts of one category are converted to one unit and others listed apart; container types and preservation conditions come from configurable lists; the total is guidance, and a sample in several containers is recorded in the amount received, such as "3 × 1 L bottles".
- **Holding extra samples.** How long are extra samples held while the revised quotation is unpaid, and what if it is declined or the samples spoil? Proposed answer, not decided: at most N days, by the sample type's preservation condition; once declined or overdue they are closed as not ordered, then returned or disposed of with a record.
- **Collections without a signature or without samples.** Can a collection task be done while its handover record is unsigned, and how is a trip with no sample closed? Proposed answer, not decided: the task cannot be done while its handover record is unsigned; a trip with no sample cancels the task with a reason and tells sales.
- **Customer refuses to sign.** What happens when the customer refuses to sign the handover record? See also "Samples without the customer present" in [Laboratory questions](laboratory.md). Proposed answer, not decided: the refusal is recorded with a reason and photos and the sample delivery staff sign to confirm; the samples are received but not handed over to departments until sales agrees with the customer; the record's legal value is for the owner to decide.
- **Custody history.** How is a move of a sample, such as to another fridge, recorded? Proposed answer, not decided: every change of a sample's holder or location is a custody entry (from, to, person, time, condition) that is never edited or deleted; the sample's current holder and location are those of its latest entry.
- **Disposal reminders.** Do samples not kept, and samples of cancelled requests, appear on the disposal reminder, and who receives it? Proposed answer, not decided: both join the list to dispose of from that day; each sample gets one task in the queue of the department holding it, or of testers when none does.
- **Changed retention days.** Does changing a sample type's retention days change the retention end of samples already retained? Proposed answer, not decided: no; a retained sample keeps the date it got, like a copied limit.
- **Disposal holds.** Can a sample be disposed of while a change request is open? Proposed answer, not decided: no; a sample cannot be disposed of while a change request on one of its results or on its request's report is open, or while it waits for the customer's agreement; the hold reason is shown.

## Testing and results

- **Subcontractor accreditation lapsing.** What happens when the subcontractor of an accepted quotation loses its accreditation before the dispatch? Proposed answer, not decided: as for a machine fault, no dispatch is made; the laboratory waits for the renewal, or sales tells the customer and the way of testing changes only through a revised quotation.
- **Machines and departments.** Which department manages a machine and its bookings? Proposed answer, not decided: a machine names the department that manages it, so department permissions apply to it and its bookings.

## Reports and laboratory files

- **Undelivered reports.** What happens when a report's email bounces or nobody collects it? Proposed answer, not decided: a bounced email puts the report back on the list to deliver, with the reason; a report not collected in person after a configured number of days reminds the customer; the portal download stays available.
- **Raw data and machines.** Is a raw-data version linked to the machine or booking that produced it? Proposed answer, not decided: an optional link from a raw-data version to that machine or booking.
- **Turning off raw-data folders.** Are saves between syncs lost when a raw-data folder is turned off during testing? Proposed answer, not decided: a raw-data folder cannot be turned off while its sample is being tested, or the computer keeps a local copy of every save.
- **Names that reveal the customer.** May file names, and printed subcontract dispatches, name the customer? See also "Anonymity beyond the label" in [Laboratory questions](laboratory.md). Proposed answer, not decided: sample folders are named by lab code, and file names follow a convention set through sync rules, with a warning or a refusal for the owner to choose; a printed dispatch shows only the lab code, sample type, parameters, methods and due date, never the customer, order or price.
