# Laboratory questions

- **Result content.** Which machine measured a result, who measured it and when, and whether the result passes or fails its limit.
- **Sample sign-off.** Does a sample have its own approval chain on top of its results' chains?
- **Change requests.** [SH-07](../docs/functional/shared.md#sh-07-signed-documents-are-locked) change requests on signed documents need their own records: requested change, reason, status and the new version they produce.
