# E-commerce questions

- **Refunds.** If a customer paid an advance and the order is cancelled, who refunds and how is it recorded? (blocks US-S16, US-S17)
- **Credit notes.** How is a posted invoice corrected with a credit note? (blocks US-A02)
- **Customer templates.** Can customers save their own orders as templates in the portal, or does only sales create templates for them? (blocks US-S09)
- **Sales data retention.** Orders, quotations and invoices older than a year could be archived and removed, keeping the last year in the system for trends. Which records, after how long, and does Vietnamese accounting law require keeping invoices longer in the system?
- **Fixed-amount discounts.** For now discounts are percentages only, as [Sales](../docs/functional/ecommerce_module/features/sales.md#2-quotation-us-s05-to-us-s10-us-s21-us-s22) says. Will sales later need a fixed amount off a line or off the whole quotation?
- **Tax rates over time.** An Odoo tax has one rate. A change of rate for a period, such as VAT going from 10% to 8%, is a new tax put on the products for that period, and lines already priced keep their tax. Is that enough, or must the rate follow the quotation date by itself? (blocks US-AD18)
- **Tax of a line.** A parameter price and a package carry a MediLab tax, while an order line takes an Odoo tax from its product, and nothing links the two: a line added on the order form has no tax, and the demo sets taxes by hand. Which Odoo tax does a MediLab tax stand for on a line, and is it set when the price or package is saved? (blocks US-AD17, US-S06)
- **Package revenue over time.** The revenue report spreads a package line over its parameters by the package's parameters and prices of today, so a later change to a package or a parameter price moves past revenue between groups. Is that acceptable, or must each invoice line keep the spread of the day it was invoiced? (US-A14)
- **Support request deadline.** A support request's task is due 24 elapsed hours after it reaches the salesperson, nights and weekends included, and the administrator can change the default on the task type. This is the provisional default; it awaits formal confirmation, including whether only working hours should count. (blocks US-S20)
- **Undelivered notice task.** When is the task to contact a customer about an undelivered mandatory notice due, and is it cancelled when its order is cancelled? (blocks US-S24)
- **Priority surcharge.** A priority order makes its tests urgent. Does it cost more, and is the surcharge a line on the quotation?
- **Quotation layout.** What does the laboratory's báo giá show, and in what order: the header, the customer and contact, the salesperson, the lines by sample, the totals, the advance payment, the payment deadline and the signatures? A sample of the current paper form settles it. (blocks US-S07, asked of @nnh53)
- **PayOS checkout transaction.** Must the first payment request redirect directly to a newly created PayOS link, or may it show the pending page while a committed background job creates the link? Direct creation needs a transaction boundary that releases the invoice lock before the external call and preserves request retry and duplicate-click guarantees.
- **Catalog filter size.** Must the catalog offer every sample type and parameter group, including those without an offered product, or should those controls search or page a restricted set? A fixed result limit would hide existing choices.
- **Quotation and posting task deadlines.** A quotation task is due 24 elapsed hours after the order is submitted, and an invoice posting task 24 elapsed hours after the invoice is drafted, as for support requests. Are these the right defaults, and should only working hours count? (blocks US-S01, US-A02)
- **Quotation task for sales orders.** An order sales creates on behalf of a customer is claimed at once and gets no quotation task. Should it get one in its salesperson's to-do list, so every unsent quotation is a task? (blocks US-S02)
- **Orders already in the queue.** Orders submitted before quotation tasks existed have no task. Should the upgrade create one for each order still waiting in the queue or claimed and not quoted yet? (blocks US-S01)
- **Reorder of an order in progress.** The portal offers Reorder on every order except a draft, which is still the cart, as Reorder's source rule allows any order that is not completed. Should an order still in progress, submitted, quoted or in testing, also not offer it, so only completed and cancelled orders do? (US-C10)
- **Other waiting steps as tasks.** Which of these become tasks, for whom, when are they done, and with what deadline and schedule? (blocks SH-10)
  - A cash or transfer payment sales recorded, waiting for the accountant to confirm or reject it (US-A08).
  - A quotation above the Head of Sales's thresholds, waiting for them to approve it before it is sent (US-S07).
  - A sent quotation not paid by its payment deadline, waiting for its salesperson to follow up, extend it or let it expire (US-S10).
  - A completed order with something left to invoice, waiting for the accountant to create its final invoice (US-A02).
  - A posted invoice past its due date, waiting for the accountant to follow up beyond the automatic reminders (US-A12).
  - A support request about no order, waiting in the queue until a salesperson takes it (US-S20).
  - An account flagged as a possible duplicate, waiting for sales to clear or merge it (US-S04).
  - A day of PayOS transactions, waiting for the accountant to reconcile and lock it (US-A13).

- **Head of Sales approving their own quotation.** Who approves a quotation above the thresholds when the only Head of Sales prepared it? Proposed answer, not decided: another salesperson asks for the approval, or the administrator sets a substitute approver in the chain.
- **Measuring approval thresholds.** Is the discount threshold measured per line or on the whole quotation, and before or after VAT? Proposed answer, not decided: the discount share is (catalog total − total after every discount) / catalog total before VAT, worked out for the whole quotation and for each line, taking the larger; the amount threshold is compared with the total after discounts, VAT included.
- **Price changes before quoting.** Which price applies when the catalog changes between submitting an order and pricing its quotation? Proposed answer, not decided: an order line keeps the price of when it was submitted; a draft quotation warns when the catalog price has changed; a sent quotation keeps its prices.
- **Packages with untestable parameters.** Is a package offered when one of its parameters has no way of testing available? Proposed answer, not decided: a package is shown only when each of its parameters has an available way of testing; archiving a parameter warns the administrator about the packages that contain it.
- **Reorders when a way of testing is gone.** Do reorders and order templates keep the chosen way of testing, and what replaces one that is gone? Proposed answer, not decided: the original is kept when available; otherwise a line whose way the customer chose is dropped with a notice, and a line on the default takes today's default with a notice.
- **Duplicate open orders.** Is sales told when a customer has an identical open order? Proposed answer, not decided: nothing is blocked; claiming an order warns when the customer has an open order with the same content, so sales can ask the customer.
- **A salesperson who leaves.** Who takes over a leaver's orders, requests and pending payments, and who keeps the KPIs? Proposed answer, not decided: the Head of Sales reassigns their orders; support requests follow their order, and the others go back to the shared queue; pending payments stay valid for the accountant; KPIs keep the person in charge at each step.
- **An individual's tax code.** Is an individual customer's tax code their citizen ID? Proposed answer, not decided: the individual enters the citizen ID, and the tax code is filled in with it and can be changed; to confirm with the accountant under Thông tư 86/2024/TT-BTC.
- **Several portal users of one business customer.** May every portal user of a business customer pay, decline quotations and cancel orders? Proposed answer, not decided: every portal account of the customer has full rights on its documents; rights per person, such as only the customer's accountant paying, are for the owner to decide.
- **Collection notices.** Does "collection" on the portal mean picking up samples or results, and is the customer told about appointments and rejected samples? Proposed answer, not decided: the order's contact is told when a collection is scheduled, changed or cancelled, receives the signed handover record by email after each handover, and is told when a sample is rejected, through their preferred channels.
- **Editing a submitted order.** May a customer edit an order already submitted, before or after sales claims it, and which fields? Proposed answer, not decided: the customer edits it while it is submitted and unclaimed; once claimed, the portal shows it read-only and changes go through sales; on their own draft, a customer changes only its lines (parameter, chosen way of testing, package, quantity), contact and notes, which the API enforces too; editing and claiming take the same record lock.
- **Portal users who leave the customer.** Who revokes the portal account of a person who leaves the customer's company? Proposed answer, not decided: the customer asks sales or support; the Head of Sales revokes the account at once and keeps its history; an account never moves to another company.
- **Login and contact email.** Is the login email separate from the contact email, and is it verified? Proposed answer, not decided: they are separate; changing the login email is confirmed through the new address; notices and password reset links go only to a confirmed address.
- **Requested date.** Is the customer's requested date required, and what if the committed return date has already passed when the samples are received? Proposed answer, not decided: the requested date is required when the test request is confirmed; a committed date already past when the samples are received marks the tests urgent at once and tells the head of department.
- **What customers see of ways of testing.** What do customers see of ways of testing and subcontracting when they order? Proposed answer, not decided: parameters, groups, sample types, the parameter's price and the methods offered, marked "performed by a subcontractor" without its name; never departments, machines, chemicals or subcontractor names.
