# Inventory questions

- **Inventory roles.** Who maintains items, locations and suppliers, who creates purchase orders, who receives goods and who disposes of expired lots: a storekeeper, the administrator, or each department?
- **Purchase approval chain.** Which roles sign a purchase order, and does the chain depend on its amount?
- **Who is warned.** Who receives the below-minimum, expiring and expired warnings: the storekeeper, the department that holds the location, or both?
- **Counts.** Does the laboratory count its stock periodically, and is a count recorded as one document with corrections for each item?
