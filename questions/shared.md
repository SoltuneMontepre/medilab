# Shared questions

- **Withdrawing after losing access.** A signer withdraws their own signature or rejection; the documents say nothing more. Withdrawing does not check again that the person holds the `sign` permission and the role of the level, and it reads the document only where its type's completion rule does. May a signer who has lost the permission, the role or access to the document still withdraw, or is withdrawing refused like signing? (US-HD16, SH-04)
