# Approval and signing chain

- **Approving is signing.** Both mean "I allow this to proceed and I take responsibility for it". There is one concept, not two.
- For the user, signing is one click of a button.
- When a document or report is generated, the signatures of the people its approval rules name are attached automatically.
- A signed document is locked: it cannot be changed or discarded, even by its owner.
- Until the document's process is completed, a signer at any level, the final level included, can withdraw their own signature once every higher level has withdrawn theirs.
- Once a result, report, quotation or order completes its process, no signature can be withdrawn; changes use a change request producing a linked version. Posted invoices use adjustment invoices or credit notes instead.
- The Head of Department approves results; the Lab Head approves instead when the entrant is the department's only result signer. Nobody approves a result they entered. The Lab Head signs the report.
- A correction to a result in an issued report automatically links or creates its report change request. A report awaiting signature returns to draft before corrected results are applied. After required approvals, a fresh signing task releases the corrected report; only release marks the previous report replaced.
- Each signature keeps its own record of who signed and at what level when it was applied. A later change to the signer's role or account does not change an existing signature.
