# Lab head

The lab head keeps the list of parameters each person may test and assigns and books the tasks of every department.

## Parameter authorizations

- Adds, changes and removes the parameters each person is authorized to test. Nobody else changes them; every internal user reads them.
- Removing an authorization keeps the person's existing assignments and bookings, but they are no longer assigned, claim or are booked for that parameter.

## Tasks

- Reads and assigns the tasks of every department, and moves a task to another person or department with a reason.
- Claims the testing task of any department's sample test they are authorized to test. Anyone who assigns the tasks of every department claims them the same way.

## Bookings

- Reads, approves, moves and cancels the machine bookings of every department, and approves or rejects the moves of its plan revisions.

## Results

- Enters, changes and signs the results of the tests they claimed or were assigned, as a tester does.
