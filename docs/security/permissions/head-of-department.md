# Head of department

The head of department runs the testing work of their department: its tasks, machine bookings, OverTime, sample handovers and the approval of its results.

## Tasks

- Reads, creates and assigns the tasks of their department, and moves a task to another person or department with a reason.
- Still reads a task they moved to another department, but no longer changes it; its form stays open, read-only.
- Reads, creates, moves and cancels the machine bookings of their department's tasks.
- Reads, records, changes and deletes the planned OverTime of their department's testers.
