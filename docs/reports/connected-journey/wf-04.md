# WF-04 Result entry and approval: connected journey plan

## Position

Story step 11. It follows SF-05, which leaves the journey booking scheduled again on a machine in use, and precedes
WF-05, which outsources the sample's second test. The journey walks the main path without result files
(`Start booking` → `files? no` → `Enter result with snapshot unit and LOD/LOQ` → `comparison requested? no` →
`Check measurement and any sanity warning` → `Send and sign` → `Notify the head` → `approve` → `Every remaining test
approved? no` → `Wait for other tests`), with WF-04-A4 (no requested comparison, no conclusion) and WF-04-A2 (the
tester withdraws the sent result before the head acts and sends it again). WF-04-E2 shows only as the check that no
warning appears (see finding 6). Files (F-53 to F-57, WF-04-A6 to A9, E4 to E6) are out of the click journey: one
step shows the empty Results folder and that the browser cannot add a file; see finding 2.

## Connection findings

Confirmed on the live app unless marked otherwise.

1. **A booking starts ahead of its slot, and the result then starts in the future.** `Start` checks only that the
   booking is scheduled and whose it is. Clicked hours before the slot, the booking turns In progress and keeps its
   planned start and end; the result's `Started At` defaults to that planned start, so a result entered and approved at
   07:10 says it started at 09:15 the same day. The tester may correct the field by hand; nothing asks her to. The
   spec keeps the default and shows it with a soft check, red while the slot is in the future.
2. **Result files cannot be added from the browser.** The tester reaches the sample's Results folder from the sample
   (`Folders`), sees it empty and has no line to add and no upload field; content arrives only through Medilab Sync
   or the ONLYOFFICE editor. So `files? yes`, the head's signing of a result-file version and WF-04-A6 to A9, E4 to E6
   are not walked. The spec shows the gap with a soft check, red.
3. **Starting the booking is optional.** Saving a result takes the test's scheduled or in-progress booking and sets
   it Done, and puts the test in Testing by itself. The journey clicks `Start` to walk the documented path.
4. **The machine has no busy state.** After `Start` the machine's form still says In use; "busy" is only the
   booking In progress, "available" the booking Done. The booking turns Done when the result is saved, before it is
   sent, not on "submitting" as business-flows.md says.
5. **The testing task follows the sending, not the booking.** The task stays Assigned in My Tasks while the booking
   is In progress and after the result is saved; it never shows In progress unless the tester presses the task's own
   `Start`, a different button on the task form. Sending the result sets it Done; withdrawing the result reopens the
   same task as Assigned, the test returns to Testing and the booking stays Done; sending again closes it again.
6. **Sending and approving are plain clicks.** `Send for Approval` and `Approve` ask no password, PIN or
   confirmation. A withdrawn signature stays in the result's Signatures with its withdrawal time.
7. **The head is told in the application only after choosing so.** Demo users are notified by e-mail by default;
   with that setting the head's messaging menu has no notices, and the audit log of the sample records that the
   e-mail was not sent ("Connection failed (outgoing mail server problem)", or "has no portal account and no usable
   email address"). The journey's head first chooses `In Odoo` in the preferences; the notice then arrives, once for
   each sending, and a click on it opens the result form. Without it, the Tasks dashboard tile To approve →
   `Results to approve` is the only signal; it shows the result as soon as it is sent and drops it on withdrawal.
8. **Records open in dialogs from the sample test.** A click on a row of the Bookings or Results tab opens the
   booking or the result in a dialog over the sample test, with its buttons (`Send for Approval`, `Withdraw
   Signature`). The sample test behind keeps its old status and buttons until the dialog is closed.
9. **The head of department cannot open the test request.** The sample form links its test request, but the head's
   click answers "You are not allowed to access 'Test Request'"; the roles the message allows (administrator, Sales,
   Head of Sales, Lab Head, sample delivery staff) leave out the tester too. The spec reads the request's status as
   sample delivery staff.
10. **No comparison, no conclusion (WF-04-A4).** The journey's sample has no regulation: the result form shows no
    conclusion field and no Regulation card, and the head's list shows an empty Conclusion. A comparison would need a
    regulation with a limit for the parameter, chosen for the sample on the order, before the result is saved.
11. **WF-04-E2 cannot be shown with a new parameter.** The spread warning needs earlier approved results of the same
    way and sample type, and without a limit there is no limit warning; the journey asserts that no warning shows.
12. **The customer sees nothing of an approved result.** The portal order says Testing while the tests are under way; its
    lines show item and price only. Results reach the customer with the report (WF-06).
13. **Cypress shortcuts this spec replaces:** `setup.js` builds the sample, assignment, booking and start over RPC and
    sends and approves results with `callAs(... "action_sign")`; `TC-WF04-M` enters a result without starting a
    booking.
