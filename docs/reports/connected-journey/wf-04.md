## Position

Story step 11. It follows SF-05, which leaves the journey booking scheduled again on a machine in use, and precedes
WF-05, which outsources the sample's second test. The journey walks the main path without result files: the journey's
testing produces none, so the `files? yes` branch is not taken (`Start booking` → `files? no` → `Enter result with
snapshot unit and LOD/LOQ` → `comparison requested? no` → `Check measurement and any sanity warning` → `Send and sign`

## Connection findings

Confirmed on the live app unless marked otherwise. Findings 1, 2, 6, 7, 9 and 11 are observations the chain no longer
checks.

1. **A booking starts ahead of its slot, and the result then starts in the future.** `Start` checks only that the
   booking is scheduled and whose it is. Clicked hours before the slot, the booking turns In progress and keeps its
   planned start and end; the result's `Started At` defaults to that planned start, so a result entered and approved at
   07:10 says it started at 09:15 the same day. The tester may correct the field by hand; nothing asks her to.
2. **Result files cannot be added from the browser.** The sample's Results folder (sample → `Folders`) has no line to
   add and no upload field; content arrives only through Medilab Sync or the ONLYOFFICE editor. The journey produces
   no file, so the `files? yes` branch, the head's signing of a result-file version and WF-04-A6 to A9, E4 to E6 are
   not walked.
3. **Starting the booking is optional.** Saving a result takes the test's scheduled or in-progress booking and sets
   it Done, and puts the test in Testing by itself. The journey clicks `Start`, the documented start.
4. **The machine has no busy state, and it is freed before submitting.** After `Start` the machine's form still says
   In use; "busy" is only the booking In progress, as `docs/specs/scheduling.md:119` allows. The booking turns Done
   when the result is saved, before it is sent, not on submitting as business-flows.md and `tester.md` (Releasing the
   machine) say. The spec checks the booking Done after sending.
5. **The testing task follows the sending, not the booking.** The task stays Assigned in My Tasks while the booking
   is In progress and after the result is saved. Sending the result sets it Done.
6. **Sending and approving are plain clicks.** `Send for Approval` and `Approve` ask no password, PIN or
   confirmation.
7. **The head is told by e-mail, which the stack cannot send.** Demo users keep Odoo's e-mail preference, so the
   mandatory `test_result.sent` goes by e-mail (step 7, outside); the audit log records that the e-mail was not sent
   (outgoing mail server). The head finds the result on the Tasks dashboard tile `Results to approve`
   (`docs/functional/shared.md:225`), which lists it as soon as it is sent. A head who chooses `In Odoo` gets the
   notice in the messaging menu after a reload, and a click on it opens the result.
8. **Records open in dialogs from the sample test.** A click on a row of the Results tab opens the result in a dialog
   over the sample test, with its buttons; the sample test behind keeps its old status until the dialog is closed.
9. **The head of department cannot open the test request.** The sample form links its test request, but the head's
   click answers "You are not allowed to access 'Test Request'".
10. **No comparison, no conclusion.** The journey's sample has no regulation: the result form shows no conclusion
    field and no Regulation card, and the head's list shows an empty Conclusion.
11. **WF-04-E2 cannot be shown with a new parameter.** The spread warning needs earlier approved results of the same
    way and sample type, and without a limit there is no limit warning; the journey checks that no warning shows.
12. **Withdrawing (WF-04-A2) works.** Walked by the earlier journey: the tester's `Withdraw Signature` before the head
    acts returns the result to Draft, drops it from `Results to approve`, reopens the testing task as Assigned, and
    sending again notifies the head again. It is not in the chain.
