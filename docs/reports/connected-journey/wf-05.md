# WF-05 Outsourced testing: connected journey plan

## Position

Story step 12. It follows WF-04, which leaves the in-house test `Journey nitrate {TAG}` approved and the sample's
second test, **Lead (Pb)**, untouched in the Chemistry queue. It precedes SF-01 and WF-06: approving the lead result

## Connection findings

1. **The subcontractor's result does not go to the head; the flow breaks here.** business-flows.md WF-05 ("the head
   is notified and approves them like any other result") and `overview.md` (Entered by: "A subcontractor's result goes
   to approval when entered") disagree with the app. The result entered on the portal stays Draft (status `none`),
   so `Results to approve` does not list it and nobody sees "Approve". When the head presses "Send for Approval", the
   head signs at the Tester level and can no longer approve (`test_result.py` `_approval_signs_again`); a tester of
   the department sending it lets the head approve, which the earlier journey did and which is no business step. No
   notice is raised when the result is entered: the head's only notice is `subcontract_dispatch.received`, by e-mail.
   The spec fails hard at step 8.
2. **WF-01 must accept the ICP-MS method, not the AAS default.** On bottled water the default lead way is AAS and no
   subcontractor tests lead by AAS; with AAS accepted the Outsource button is hidden.
3. **The dispatch is chosen by the system, not by the head.** The outsource dialog has no choice of dispatch: the test
   joins the subcontractor's oldest draft dispatch, or a new one when there is none (`sample_test.py`
   `_send_to_subcontractor`). Northern has no draft dispatch on the demo database, so the journey's test gets one of
   its own; the spec finds its row by lab code.
4. **The lead test is always suggested for outsourcing.** The suggestion is set when the head scans the sample in
   WF-02: ICPMS-01 is held by a demo measurement in progress whose end has passed (`machine_booking_conditions.py`
   `_free_slot`). With a suggestion the outsource dialog has no reason field. WF-05-A3 (straight away with a reason)
   cannot be walked with this test.
5. **Expected date.** Northern's lead way shows turnaround 3 days; the dispatch sent on a day expects its results 3
   calendar days later. The spec reads the turnaround from the way's form.
6. **The head names the carrier first.** Dispatches to Carry lists only the dispatches that name the signed-in person,
   and "Hand Over" shows only to that person. `sample-delivery-staff.md` (Carrier) gives this to the head: "The head
   chooses the sample delivery staff on the draft dispatch, and they hand it over from their list of dispatches to
   carry", so the chain walks it as a business step (step 3). Staff get no to-do for it, only the menu list.
7. **The lead test must not be started or reassigned elsewhere upstream.** Outsourcing needs status `waiting` or
   `assigned`.
8. **The customer sees nothing of the outsourcing.** The order page keeps the accepted way and names neither the
   subcontractor nor the dispatch. The subcontractor's pages show the lab code, sample type, parameter and method,
   and nothing of the customer, the order or the request.
9. **The sent dispatch shows on the head's Outsourcing Schedule** (list view) with Northern, Vo Van Tai and Sent, and
   the outsourced test leaves the department's queue. Observations, not checked by the chain.
