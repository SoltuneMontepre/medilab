# WF-05 Outsourced testing: connected journey plan

## Position

Story step 12. It follows WF-04, which leaves the in-house test `Journey nitrate {TAG}` approved and the sample's
second test, **Lead (Pb)**, untouched in the Chemistry queue. It precedes SF-01 and WF-06: approving the lead result
completes the sample and the test request, which drafts the report. The journey walks the main path: the lead test
carries the scheduler's outsourcing suggestion from the moment Chemistry scans the sample (finding 4), the head
accepts it, the test goes in a dispatch to Northern, the head names the sample delivery staff, they hand it over
(dispatch sent), the subcontractor confirms receipt and enters the result on the portal, a Chemistry tester sends it
for approval and the head approves. WF-05-A1, A2 and E1 to E4 are not walked. WF-05-A3 (outsourcing straight away
with a reason) is walked by the same clicks only when the test shows no suggestion: the dialog then asks for the
reason. The spec reads the alert on the test and follows the path the screen offers.

**Pick for the second test (align WF-01, WF-02, SF-06, SF-08):** parameter **Lead (Pb)**
(`sol_demo.demo_test_parameter_lead`), sample type **Bottled water** (`sol_demo.demo_sample_type_bottled_water`),
method chosen by the customer in the cart **ISO 17294-2:2016** (option "Lead (Pb) – ISO 17294-2:2016", which
becomes the accepted in-house Chemistry way `sol_demo.demo_parameter_method_lead_icpms`), subcontractor **Northern
Testing Laboratory** (`sol_demo.demo_subcontractor_northern_testing`, VILAS 101, subcontracted way
`sol_demo.demo_parameter_method_lead_subcontracted`), portal login **`demo.subcontractor` / `demo`** (contact Nguyen
Thi Lan). SF-06 must only keep Northern's accreditation expiry in the future.

## Connection findings

1. **The subcontractor's result does not go to the head; a tester must send it.** business-flows.md WF-05 ("the head
   is notified and approves them like any other result") and `subcontractor.md` disagree with the app. The result
   entered on the portal is Draft. The head and the tester both see "Send for Approval" on it and nobody sees
   "Approve". When the head presses "Send for Approval", the head signs at the Tester level and is left with only
   "Withdraw Signature": the head cannot approve a result they sent unless they entered it
   (`test_result.py` `_approval_signs_again`), and the tester has no button either, so only another head could
   approve. No task tells a tester or the head that the result waits; the head's only notice is the e-mail about the
   receipt of the dispatch, and nothing is raised when the result is entered. Journey: step 9 checks softly that the
   head can approve at once (red), then `demo.tester` finds the result by menu search and sends it.
2. **WF-01 must accept the ICP-MS method, not the AAS default.** On bottled water the default lead way is AAS and no
   subcontractor tests lead by AAS; with AAS accepted the Outsource button is hidden. The customer selects "Lead (Pb)
   – ISO 17294-2:2016" in the cart, which the journey's WF-01 does: the test shows it as Accepted Way of Testing.
3. **The dispatch is chosen by the system, not by the head.** The outsource dialog has no choice of dispatch: the test
   joins the subcontractor's oldest draft dispatch, or a new one when there is none (`sample_test.py`
   `_send_to_subcontractor`). On the demo database Northern has no draft dispatch (its dispatch of five lead tests is
   cancelled after a failed delivery; the only draft belongs to Saigon Analytical Laboratory), so the journey's test
   gets a dispatch of its own. The spec still finds its row by lab code and counts no rows, since a draft left by an
   interrupted run would be shared and sent with it.
4. **The lead test is always suggested for outsourcing, so WF-05-A3 cannot be walked with it.** The suggestion is set
   when the head scans the sample in WF-02: the department plan finds no ICP-MS slot, because ICPMS-01 is held by a
   demo measurement in progress whose end has passed (`machine_booking_conditions.py` `_free_slot`). "Change Due
   Date" on the test says "No machine, tester and room are free to test it within the next 30 days." With a
   suggestion the outsource dialog has no reason field, so the head cannot give a reason. The spec walks the main
   path and keeps the A3 clicks for a test without a suggestion; they were not run on the live app.
5. **Expected date.** Northern's lead way shows turnaround 3 days; the dispatch sent on a day expects its results 3
   calendar days later. The spec reads the turnaround from the way's form instead of a fixed number.
6. **Sample delivery staff cannot take a dispatch nobody gave them.** Dispatches to Carry lists only the dispatches
   that name the signed-in person, and "Hand Over" shows only to that person, so the head names the staff on the
   draft dispatch first (step 3); the business flow says only "when sample delivery staff take the dispatch". Staff
   get no to-do for it, only the menu list.
7. **The lead test must not be started or reassigned elsewhere upstream.** Outsourcing needs status `waiting` or
   `assigned`. WF-03, SF-10 and WF-04 must touch only the nitrate test.
8. **Comparison with a regulation adds a field.** If WF-01 asked for comparison, the tester must set a conclusion
   before sending (`test_result.py`). The journey's order asks for none: the tester sends without one.
9. **Draft report preparation depends on a cron.** `_open_signing_task` wakes the PDF-preparation scheduled job
   (`test_report.py:241-248`); a user cannot run it. The PDF is WF-06's concern.
10. **The customer sees nothing of the outsourcing.** The order page keeps the accepted way "Lead (Pb) – ISO
    17294-2:2016" and names neither the subcontractor nor the dispatch. The subcontractor's pages show the lab code,
    sample type, parameter and method, and nothing of the customer, the order or the request.
11. **Cypress shortcuts this plan replaces.** `setup.js` creates and receives the request and assigns the task over
    RPC, outsources through the wizard model, sets the staff and sends over RPC, confirms receipt with a posted form,
    and E1 visits `/my/dispatches/<id>` directly. Every one has a click path above.
