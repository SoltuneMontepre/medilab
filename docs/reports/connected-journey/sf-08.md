# SF-08 Laboratory setup: connected journey plan

## Position

Step 1 of the story; every later flow works on what it leaves. It runs on the demo database, not on an empty
laboratory, and walks only the parts of the SF-08 main path the story needs: the administrator creates one test
parameter `Journey nitrate {TAG}`, gives it an in-house way of testing, explicit applicability to one sample type
(unit, LOD, LOQ, decimals, default), a standard turnaround and a price with tax. The administrator then makes sure the
way's testing method states a sampling requirement for the sample type and runs on two calibrated machines in use.
These belong to the testing method, not to the parameter; the journey adds what the demo method lacks once and reuses
it on later runs. The Lab Head authorizes the demo tester (US-LH04), and a customer finds the parameter with its price
in the catalog, which is where WF-01 starts. Alternative paths SF-08-A1 to A5 are not walked: the journey needs no
package, role change, VAT change, approval chain change or template edit. No regulation limit is added (finding 4).

The spec is `src/tests/playwright/journey/01-sf-08-laboratory-setup.spec.js`.

## Data

- `{TAG}` = `J<MMDD>-<HHmmss>` of the run; parameter name `Journey nitrate {TAG}`. The code is not typed; the sequence
  gives `CT.nnnn`, read from the form in step 1.
- Way: `LAB-CR6-01`, Chemistry, turnaround 3 working days, no duration (the machine links give 60 min), capacity 1. Step
  2 of the method books the Colorimetry room P106.
- Applicability: Bottled water, mg/L, LOD 0.05, LOQ 0.15, 2 decimals, default.
- Price 180,000 VND excluding VAT, VAT 10%. One sample: 180,000 + 18,000 = 198,000 VND.
- Dates are typed in the format the field shows when clicked (`mm/dd/yyyy` for the administrator) and are days of the
  laboratory's time zone, Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh.
- Authorization: Dang Thi Lan (`demo.tester`) for `Journey nitrate {TAG}`. The head Hoang Van Minh is not authorized
  for it.

## Connection findings

1. **Sampling requirements and machine links belong to the testing method, not to the parameter, and the method of
   the journey has no sampling requirement for Bottled water in the demo.** Without one the cart shows the sample with
   "Expected samples: 0" and "Methods without a sampling requirement: LAB-CR6-01", and the order line counts no
   expected sample. Nothing warns the administrator while creating the parameter or its applicability. The spec adds the
   requirement on the method once (step 4); a second one for the same method and sample type is refused.
2. **Chemically wrong method.** No demo nitrate method exists; the story uses the chromium(VI) colorimetry method,
   whose machine has no demo booking ([SF-05](sf-05.md)), and gives its requirement Lead's container and preservation
   so the journey's sample stays one container.
3. **The catalog offers the parameter before anyone may test it.** admin.md says an in-house way is offered only when
   at least one person is authorized for its parameter. On the live app the parameter shows in the catalog with its
   price right after step 3, before the Lab Head acts (`parameter_method.py`: "Who may test a parameter is not checked
   yet"). The journey is unaffected because step 6 runs before WF-01.
4. **A regulation limit is needed for a Pass or Fail.** The journey adds none. If WF-01 orders the sample with a
   regulation comparison, the result only accepts "Insufficient Basis" (`test_result.py`).
5. **A signed-out visitor has no link to the catalog.** `/` leads to the login page, which has no link to it; the
   header entry Catalog shows to a signed-in customer, or to a visitor already on `/catalog` or `/cart`. Step 7
   therefore signs in as the demo customer.
6. **Fields of the setup screens offer to create records.** On Groups, and on Subcontractor of a way of testing, a
   typed name that matches nothing is answered with `Create "<name>"` as the first suggestion.
7. Business-flows "Starts from: an empty laboratory" does not match the journey, which starts from the demo data. No
   cron, external service or e-mail is involved in this flow.
