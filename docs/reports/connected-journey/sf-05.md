Step 3 would be outside the browser: with their default preference By Emails, a notice to the tester and the head goes by e-mail (`models/notifications/mail_message.py`, `_preferred_channels`). But the downtime sends none: saving the machine's status calls `_requeue` (`models/master_data/machine.py:101-105`), which returns the bookings to the plan by `_set` alone (`models/testing/machine_booking_conditions.py:167-179`, `models/testing/machine_booking.py:545-558`), unlike leave and an unavailable room, whose `_return_to_plan` raises `machine_booking.returned` to the head (`models/testing/machine_booking_interruptions.py:129-160`). So the spec fails the step instead of writing it outside.

Step 4 is the head's because the documents give the approval of every proposed booking to the head: "Neither the tester nor the head reserves a proposed booking without this approval" and "nobody but the head reserves a proposed booking" (`docs/functional/laboratory_module/features/head-of-department.md:55,71`), WF-03 owns approving bookings (`business-flows.md`, SF-10 Paths in other flows), and SF-05's states end "scheduled on another machine".

## Connection findings

1. **Nobody is told that the downtime returned the booking to the plan.** The main path promises notices to the tester and the head, and the head-of-department feature says downtime "notifies the head for reassignment" (`head-of-department.md:63`). No notice is created for either (step 3's evidence); they find the proposal only by searching the Machine Schedule. The tester is told only when the head approves the new slot. The chain stops here.
2. **The downtime drops the slot the head pinned.** A booking the head moved by hand (SF-10-A2) loses its pin and its slot and takes the earliest slot on the other machine.
3. **The Machine Schedule fails for bookings that keep a slot on a calibrated machine under repair.** A finished or cancelled booking keeps its slot. Working out its machine warning for such a machine raises a server error, `OverflowError: date value out of range`, and the list shows "Oops! Something went wrong". Reproduced on the live application outside the story: ICP-MS 1 put under repair, then Machine Schedule → list view without the filter Not Finished. The calendar and the list filtered on Not Finished opened. Not a step of the main path; the spec does not look.
4. **The administrator can approve the booking too.** Holding every permission, the administrator sees Approve on the proposed booking; the journey leaves the approval to the head.
5. **The status bar of a machine saves nothing by itself.** A click on Under repair or In use changes the form only; the bookings move when the form is saved.
6. **Returning to use without a calibration after the repair is refused.** Seen on an earlier run: "Record a calibration or maintenance of UV-Vis spectrophotometer 1 done after its repair on MM/DD/YYYY before putting it back in use." The business records the calibration first, so the journey does not try.

Read in the code, not walked: returning a machine to use recalculates every proposal, and a proposal still open then goes back to the first machine, whose code comes first, which is why the head approves while it is under repair; the return to use checks only the repair rule, not an expired calibration or an overdue maintenance, which block bookings instead; a booking placed on the repaired machine within 7 days shows a "was repaired on" warning.
