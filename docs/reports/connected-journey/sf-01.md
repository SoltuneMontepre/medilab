# SF-01 Invoicing and payment collection: connected journey plan

## Position

Story step 13, between WF-05 (the sample completes, the test request is `completed`) and WF-06 (the report is approved, issued, delivered and downloaded). The journey collects the balance payment request that WF-01 created when Sales confirmed the order. It walks SF-01-A1 in two parts with non-online methods: part 1 is a bank transfer the customer reports on the order page of the portal, part 2 is SF-01-A2, cash that Sales records. Before part 2 is confirmed, SF-01-E1 is walked once: the accountant rejects the cash payment with a reason, Sales reads the reason and records the payment again. The accountant confirms each part from its Payment confirmation task. Each confirmation posts its invoice. Then the customer downloads the invoices on the portal and looks for the receipt. PayOS is not reachable, so no part is paid online. SF-01-A3, SF-01-E2 and SF-01-E3 are not walked. Reconciliation is shown read-only (finding 6).

The flow needs only the confirmed order with its open balance payment request, so it also runs right after WF-01. It asserts payment facts only, not the laboratory statuses the flows between WF-01 and WF-06 change.

## Connection findings

1. **The balance holds sample collection unless WF-01 changes Balance Due.** A portal order is created with `balance_due = before_collection` (`sol_commerce/models/orders/sale_order.py:226`). With that value, confirming the order leaves the test request `confirmed`, not `paid`, hides "Start sample collection" and refuses sample intake. The docs say the opposite: "before the report" is the default, and the balance never holds collection (`docs/functional/ecommerce_module/features/sales.md:108`, `:200`, `:209`; `docs/specs/payment-requests.md:14`; business-flows WF-01 main path). The story connects because WF-01 sets Balance Due to "Before the report" in the Send quotation dialog. The mismatch between the docs and the code needs an owner decision.
2. **A full advance leaves nothing for SF-01.** No balance request is made when the order is already paid. WF-01 chooses a percentage advance (30 %).
3. **No screen links to the payment receipt.** The receipt is served only at `/my/invoices/<invoice id>/receipt/<payment id>`, as an HTML page (`sol_commerce/controllers/portal.py:119`). The invoice list, the invoice pages, the order page, the quotation page and the "Payment receipt" notice (`/my/notifications/<id>`, whose body is only "Payment of … was recorded.") have no link to it, and no screen shows the payment's id. `customer.md` ("download … the payment receipt") cannot be done by clicks. Step 15 shows it as failed.
4. **The order and its invoices are not linked on the portal.** The order page shows the balance request but no invoices, and the header has no Invoices entry. The customer reaches invoices through the user menu → My Account → "Your Invoices". The list shows number, dates, amount due and status, not the total or the order; the order number is printed inside the invoice document as Source. The journey tells the invoices apart by the numbers the accountant saw and by the total on each invoice page.
5. **PayOS (the online half of SF-01-A1).** The order page shows a PayOS button, but an online payment needs a signed PayOS webhook, which clicks cannot produce. In the Sales dialog the QR block says PayOS or the company bank account is not configured. Substitute: a bank transfer reported on the portal plus cash recorded by Sales. Both use the same accountant confirmation and the same invoice posting.
6. **Reconciliation has nothing from the journey to work on.** A reconciliation day matches PayOS transactions, which only the gateway writes. The accountant sees the demo's two days: 2 January 2020, Locked, and 3 January 2020, Open with one missing payment and the buttons Post missing payments and Lock. SF-01-E3 could be walked once on the demo's open day, but not again on the same database, and it does not touch the journey's order. The journey opens the locked day read-only (step 11).
7. **The story order hides the report hold.** SF-01 runs before WF-06, so WF-06 never shows a withheld report. To show "the issued report reaches the customer only once paid in full", split SF-01: steps 1-4 (part 1), then WF-06 up to issue, then steps 5-16. No notice tells the customer that a withheld report has been released after payment, so the customer must go back to the portal on their own.
8. **The Record payment button has no stable handle.** Its `name` is the database id of `sol_commerce.action_payment_collection`. The journey selects it by its words inside the form's status bar.
9. **Credit customers are out of scope here.** The accountant's "create the final invoice" applies only to customers on credit. `Journey Foods {TAG}` is not on credit. Not walked.
10. **The bank-transfer form names no account to pay to.** The block lists the company's bank accounts and the demo company has none, so the customer sees only the receipt time and the reference fields.
11. **SF-01-E1 tells the salesperson through a to-do that does not lead to the order.** The rejection creates a to-do on the payment, due some days later. In the Sales activity menu it is counted under "Other Activities" as a future activity, and opening that entry shows an empty list. Sales reads the reason on the order's Payment tab, in the Recorded payments table (step 7). The customer also gets a "Payment rejected" notice that quotes the accountant's reason, although Sales recorded the payment.
12. **A confirmed payment reads "Reconciled", not "Paid".** The payment form's status bar moves from Draft to Reconciled when the accountant confirms; the invoice reads Paid.
