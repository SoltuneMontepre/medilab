# SF-04 Sample retention, return and disposal: connected journey plan

## Position

Story step 16, after WF-06 (step 14) issued the report. The journey's sample is Bottled water, a type kept 30 days, so
WF-06 leaves it `Retained` and the journey walks the main path: the tester reads that the issued report retained the
sample until the report day plus the sample type's retention days; the end of the retention, the daily to-do and the
disposal come 30 days after the report, so the spec marks them `outside`. SF-04-A1 (not kept), SF-04-A2 (cancelled
request) and SF-04-A3 (return) are not walked. SF-04 does not need SF-03's replacement report.

What decides "kept or not kept" and the retention days: only the sample type. `medilab.sample.type.is_retained`
("Retained After Testing") and `retention_days`
(`src/core/modules/sol_laboratory/models/master_data/sample_type.py:51-57`). Nothing on the order, the quotation,
the test request, the reception or the sample overrides it (tester.md "Retention policy"; `sample.retained_until` is
read-only, `models/sample_collection/sample.py:185`). At issue, `_issue` sets every completed sample to `reported`,
then `_retain` moves samples of a kept type to `retained` with `retained_until = report day + retention_days`
(`models/testing/test_report_issue.py:146-148`, `models/sample_collection/sample.py:502-510`).

## Connection findings

1. **A kept sample type puts the main path and SF-04-A3 30 days after the report.** Confirmed on the live app: the
   sample is `Retained` until report day + 30. Before that day, Dispose shows on the sample form but is refused with
   "Only a sample that is not kept, whose retention is over or whose test request was cancelled can be disposed of:
   {SAMPLE_CODE}", and Return shows neither on the sample nor on the report's Samples tab (`returnable` empty). Both
   are the documented behaviour before the retention ends. The tester of the main path does not meet them: she
   disposes once the to-do lists the sample, so the spec no longer tries them. Nothing typed anywhere sets retention
   per order, request or sample.
2. **Only an upstream choice in SF-08 (step 1) would make disposal clickable in the journey**, since `_retain` reads
   the type at issue: a not-kept sample type (Surface water, the tester then types the method), Retention Days `0` on
   Bottled water (Master Data → Catalog → Sample Types, `sol_laboratory.menu_sample_type`), or "Retained After
   Testing" unticked. The last two change a shared demo record. The story keeps Bottled water with 30 days.
3. **The daily to-do is fed by a cron only.** `sol_laboratory.ir_cron_sample_retention_reminders` ("Sample retention
   reminders", daily) runs scheduled job `sample.retention.reminders`, which queues every `retained` sample whose
   `retained_until` is within the reminder days and creates a `Sample retention` task in the sample's department
   queue (`models/sample_collection/scheduled_job.py:26-48`). Cypress fires it by RPC (`setup.js` `remindRetention`).
   Confirmed: on the report day the tester's My Tasks and Department Queue hold no such task for the sample, while
   demo samples PTN.26.11.0016/0017 show theirs ("Lưu mẫu: …") in the queue.
4. **The sample form shows the sample type's disposal method only through the type's link.** The sample's own
   Disposal Method field stays empty until disposal copies the type's method into it (`sample.py:524-537`).
5. **No to-do or notice exists for a not-kept or cancelled sample.** The tester finds those in the Samples list.
6. **Cypress builds the reported sample by RPC** (`setup.js` `reportedSample()`); the journey uses the sample WF-06
   issued.
