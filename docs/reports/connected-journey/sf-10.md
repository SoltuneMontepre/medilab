## Connection findings

1. **The urgent mark moves nothing that is scheduled (SF-10-A1, seen on an earlier run).** The test turns Urgent, its approved booking keeps its slot and no plan revision is opened for it. The Chemistry list of plan revisions holds one Proposed revision of the demo data, "Makes room for urgent work: …", which names other samples.
2. **The tester is not told that the head moved the booking (SF-10-A2, seen on an earlier run).** After the head's move her notices held nothing new; she saw the new time on the calendar, on the Machine Schedule and on the Bookings tab of her test.
3. **The task list does not show the planned time.** The column Planned Start of My Tasks is hidden by default; the planned time shows on the test's Bookings tab, the Machine Schedule and the calendar.
4. **A refused move names a resource, not the cause (SF-10-A2).** The message "… is not free for … at that time." is the same for a taken machine, a tester outside working time and a start in the past.
5. **The head's pin does not survive a downtime of the machine.** SF-05 returns the booking to the plan, which clears the pin and gives it the earliest slot on the other machine (`machine_booking.py:287-290`).
6. **The plan's order cannot be checked on a screen.** The Machine Schedule shows each booking's priority class and due date, but not the expected return date or arrival that also order the plan, nor the free intervals before a booking; the head sees that the booking holds a free slot, not that it is the earliest.

Read in the code, not walked: a move past the internal due date is accepted and only flags the test at risk; the reminder before the start is sent by a scheduled job; a plan revision needs urgent work that only scheduled slots can serve, which the journey's data does not give.
