## Data

- Reason of the urgent mark: `Khách hàng cần kết quả gấp {TAG}`.
- New start: the spec tries each full hour after the booking's start inside the working blocks 08:00 to 12:00 and 13:00 to 17:00 of the working days, in order, and keeps the first one the save accepts. On the run the booking went from 10:00 to 11:00 of the same day at the first try.
- The room's reservation starts some quarter hours after the booking, 15 minutes for LAB-CR6-01; the spec reads that delay before the move and expects it after.

## Connection findings

1. **The urgent mark moves nothing that is scheduled.** The test turns Urgent, its approved booking keeps its slot and no plan revision is opened for it. The Chemistry list of plan revisions holds one Proposed revision of the demo data, "Makes room for urgent work: …", which names other samples; the journey approves nothing.
2. **The tester is not told that the head moved the booking.** After the move her notices hold nothing new; she sees the new time on the calendar, on the Machine Schedule and on the Bookings tab of her test.
3. **The task list does not show the planned time.** The column Planned Start of My Tasks is hidden by default; the spec reads the new time on the test's Bookings tab.
4. **A refused move names a resource, not the cause.** The message "… is not free for … at that time." is the same for a taken machine, a tester outside working time and a start in the past. Not met on the run, where the first slot was free.
5. **The head's pin does not survive a downtime of the machine.** SF-05 returns the booking to the plan, which clears the pin and gives it the earliest slot on the other machine.

Read in the code, not walked: a move past the internal due date is accepted and only flags the test at risk; the reminder before the start is sent by a scheduled job; a plan revision needs urgent work that only scheduled slots can serve, which the journey's data does not give.
