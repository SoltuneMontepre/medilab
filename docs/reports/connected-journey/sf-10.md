# SF-10 Scheduling plan and revisions: connected journey plan

## Position

Story step 9, between WF-03 (the head approved the plan's booking for `Journey nitrate {TAG}`) and SF-05 (the booked machine goes under repair). The journey walks SF-10-A1 (the tester marks the test urgent with a reason; it is already urgent by priority class because SF-07 accepted the priority order, so the mark adds the tester's reason but not a new class) and SF-10-A2 (the head moves the scheduled booking to another free slot through its form, which pins it and moves the tester's calendar event and room reservations with it). It ends with the booking scheduled and not started. SF-10-A4 (reminder) can be seen only when the notification cron runs; SF-10-A3, A5 and E1 to E4 are not walked (see Connection findings 5 to 9).

## Connection findings

1. **SF-07 priority and SF-10-A1 overlap.** A test of an accepted priority order is already in class `urgent` (`_compute_priority`, `sample_test_priority.py:68-77`, checks `sample_id.request_id.priority`). "Mark Urgent" is still offered (`invisible="not can_mark_urgent or marked_urgent"`, `views/testing/sample_test_views.xml:52-65`) and only adds the reason and the marker; "Remove Urgent Mark" afterwards leaves the badge "Urgent". On the journey, A1 therefore changes no placement and no booking; the check is the reason field, not the class. To prove A1 changes the class, the journey would need a test that is not a priority order (none in the story).
2. **Marking urgent does not touch a scheduled booking.** `_mark_urgent` writes `marked_urgent`, which recalculates the department plan (`sample_test_priority.py:63-66`), but `_plan` never moves scheduled bookings and `_revise` only acts on urgent *proposed* bookings (`machine_booking_revision.py:36-50`). Consequence: if any demo Chemistry proposed booking is urgent, a plan revision may appear that would move the journey booking while it is still unpinned (between steps 3 and 9). It is applied only when the head approves it; the journey must not approve a revision. Doing step 9 (pin) protects the booking: a pinned booking is reported as a conflict, never moved.
3. **The head cannot move the booking to now or to the past.** The earliest slot is now rounded up to the next quarter hour (`machine_booking.py:668-669`); a time in the past is refused, and the message names the machine ("`{MACHINE}` is not free for … at that time."), not the time (`_slot_availability`, `machine_booking.py:333-395`), which is misleading. The slot must also fit the tester's working interval, so after 17:00 local or on a weekend the earliest start is the next working day 08:00. This does **not** block WF-04: `action_start` checks only `status == 'scheduled'` (`machine_booking.py:434-439`), so the tester can press Start on the booking before `T1`. WF-04 should not wait for `T1`. A missed booking (start passed, not started) shows "Missed" and a `Reschedule` button (`action_reschedule`, `machine_booking_missed.py:46-75`), which takes the earliest free slot and pins it when the head does it; this is the fallback when WF-03's `T0` has passed before SF-10 runs, and the move of step 8 also works on a missed booking.
4. **A move past the internal due date is accepted.** `_move` checks only availability (`machine_booking.py:397-424`); a later slot sets `at_risk` and sends `sample_test.at_risk` to tester and head (`sample_test_priority.py:150-175`) instead of being refused. With a priority order, SF-07's internal due date may be today or tomorrow, so `T1` must stay early or the test turns at risk; the booking form's red "At Risk" badge is the check.
5. **The tester is not told of the head's manual move.** `_move` sends no notification; only an applied plan revision sends `machine_booking.moved` (`plan_revision_move.py:161-180`). The tester learns of the new time only from the calendar, the task's planned times or the reminder.
6. **SF-10-A4 reminders depend on a cron.** `ir_cron_notifications_remind` runs every 5 minutes (`sol_laboratory/data/ir_cron_data.xml:3-10`) and queues reminders for events starting within the lead (15 min, `constants/settings.py:6`); the old reminder is skipped because its item key carries the old start (`models/notifications/scheduled_job.py:72-77`). "Send immediately inside the warning window" means "at the next cron run", not on save. Visible in a journey only if the cron runs on the live app and `T1` is within ~20 minutes; otherwise out of scope.
7. **SF-10-A3 (cancel test)** is clickable (`Cancel Test` on the sample test form, `groups=permission_sample_test_edit_all`) but ends the journey's test; the story has no second in-house test to cancel. Not walked.
8. **SF-10-A5 (early machine release)** belongs to WF-04: entering/submitting the result or `Done` (`action_done`) on the booking. Not in SF-10's step.
9. **SF-10-E1 to E4 need conditions the journey lacks.** E1 (plan revision, menu `sol_laboratory.menu_plan_revision`, `action_approve`/`action_reject` in `plan_revision.py:118-142`) needs an urgent *proposed* booking that only scheduled bookings' slots can serve: a second received test in Chemistry, and "each affected department" needs a second loaded department. E2 needs such a revision whose move passes a due date plus Sales' new date (SF-07). E3's initial risk notice is immediate, but the daily summary is a cron and the overrun notice (`machine_bookings.overrun`, every 15 min) needs time passing. E4 needs a leave entry for demo.tester or a location unavailability; it would return the journey booking to the plan (proposed) and need a new head approval, so it competes with SF-05 and is not walked.
10. **Demo AAS-01 is held forever.** `demo_machine_booking_lead_running` is `in_progress` on AAS-01 from 02:00 to 02:30 UTC of the install day (`sol_demo/data/sol_laboratory/machine_booking.xml:13-20`); a measurement past its end holds its machine (`machine_booking_conditions.py:134-146`), so AAS-01 takes no new booking and no move. SF-08 must not rely on AAS-01 alone; with TCVN 7602 the plan uses AAS-02.
11. **Documentation mismatch.** `docs/reports/wf-03-test-plan.md:1080` lists no coverage for SF-10-A1 and `src/tests/cypress/e2e/README.md:108` says A1 depends on unbuilt features, but A1 is built and covered by `TC-F422-2` (`features/laboratory/department-plan/department-plan.cy.js`). Plan revisions (`plan_revision.py`, `machine_booking_revision.py`) and pinning are built, whereas the test plan (`wf-03-test-plan.md:21`) still calls them TARGET.
12. **No RPC is needed for the walked path.** The Cypress specs set up the "free slot" by cancelling a second booking over RPC (`booking-rescheduling.cy.js:16-22`) and read the result over RPC; the journey instead tries candidate slots on the form and reads the result on the form, list, calendar and task.
