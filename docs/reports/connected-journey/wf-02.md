# WF-02 Sample reception and handover: connected journey plan

## Position

Story step 6, between WF-01 (confirmed portal order, advance verified, test request Paid) and SF-11 / WF-03 (folders and assignment on the received sample). The spec `src/tests/playwright/journey/05-wf-02-sample-reception.spec.js` walks the WF-02 main path: the customer brings the one sample; sample delivery staff record the actual handover against the expected samples on a handover record, then the condition, receipt and storage on the sample; the customer signs the handover record on Medilab Mobile, outside the browser; staff print the label and hand the sample to its department; the head of department scans the label. WF-02-A3 does not apply, as both tests belong to Chemistry; no exception path is walked.

## Connection findings

1. **The handover record is signed only on Medilab Mobile.** The documents put the customer's signature on the staff phone (`medilab-mobile.md:97`), so the journey writes it as an outside step. In the browser the handover record form has no button at all and its fields Signed At, Signed on Device, Signature, PDF and Sent At are read-only; the portal shows no handover record, neither on the order page nor under My account. The journey's record stays Draft, the customer gets no PDF of it, and nothing downstream checks it.
2. **The handover record is not reachable from the request or the sample.** Staff open Handover Records → New and pick the request and the sample again; Received By is not prefilled with the signed-in staff. Once saved, the sample shows the record's line in its tab Handover Records.
3. **The order of the main path is not enforced.** The handover record, the receipt and the labels are independent: a sample is received without any handover record, and the department's handover row and the two testing tasks exist from the receipt on, before the customer signs, the label is printed or the head scans. The scan only records the department's receipt, which WF-03 needs before booking.
4. **The screen asks no receipt time.** Received At is the moment Receive is pressed; only the handover record has a time to edit, Handed Over At, prefilled with now.
5. **The test request's form keeps showing Paid after the receipt.** The sample is received in a dialog over the request; after the dialog closes, the sample's row shows Received but the request's status bar stays on Paid until the request is opened again, where it shows Sample Received.
6. **The printed label cuts its text.** Print Labels downloads a PDF of one 50 × 30 mm label per sample. The QR code is whole, but the three lines beside it run past the right edge: the lab code, the request code and the Due date lose their last characters. A head of department without a scanner cannot read the lab code to type.
7. **Nothing tells sample delivery staff that a paid request expects its sample.** Their Department Queue is empty; they find the request by searching the list. The request shows no order number, so the search goes by the customer (WF-01 finding 3).
8. **The expected return date does not change at receipt (SF-07).** The request and the customer's order keep the date promised on the order, counted from the pickup day Sales set, also when the sample arrives earlier. The testing tasks' deadline and the label's Due date are the tests' due date, which is a day earlier in the run. Not checked by the spec.
9. **The portal does not show that the sample was received.** The order page stays on Waiting until testing starts, with the same expected return date; it names no sample status and no receipt day. Not checked by the spec.
10. **The scan opens again after each label.** The head closes the dialog before going on; a second scan of the same label is not walked.
