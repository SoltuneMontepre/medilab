# WF-01 Order to confirmed test request: connected journey plan

## Position

Story step 4, between SF-09 part 1 (the new portal customer) and WF-02 (sample reception). The spec `src/tests/playwright/journey/04-wf-01-order-to-test-request.spec.js` walks the WF-01 main path from a portal cart. The SF-07 main path runs inside it: the customer reads the standard turnaround and gives a requested date on the cart, and Sales sees the estimated return date beside it on the quotation; those steps are named `SF-07: …`. No priority is requested: SF-07-A1 is a test of its own ([SF-07](sf-07.md), spec 04a). The advance is paid by bank transfer through **WF-01-A4** (Sales records the transfer, the accountant confirms it), because PayOS cannot be reached. Sales leaves the Send quotation dialog's balance due at what it proposes, before the report: the main path does not have Sales choose it. No Head of Sales approval is asked for: the threshold approval of the main path and WF-01-E3 is not built (finding 2). The other paths (WF-01-A1, A2, A3, A5, E1, E2, E3) are not walked; SF-02 walks WF-01-A3 later.

## Data

- `{TAG}` = `J<MMDD-HHmmss>` of the run.
- Customer: `Journey Foods {TAG}` and its default contact `Journey Buyer {TAG}`, from SF-09.
- Lines (quantity 1 each, one sample):
  - `Journey nitrate {TAG}` on Bottled water, SF-08's in-house way LAB-CR6-01, 180,000 VND, VAT 10 %, turnaround 3 working days.
  - Lead (Pb) on Bottled water, way `Lead (Pb) – ISO 17294-2:2016`, 280,000 VND as the cart shows it, VAT 10 %.
- Requested date: today + 14 days, moved to Monday when that is a Saturday or Sunday. Expected sample pickup: the next day from Monday to Friday.
- Quotation: Bank transfer, Percentage, 30; balance due and payment deadline as the dialog proposes (before the report; three days ahead); no discount.
- Amounts of the run: total including VAT 506,000; advance 151,800 (30 % of the total including VAT); balance 354,200. Record payment: amount as prefilled, Bank transfer, received now, reference `Journey advance {TAG}`.
- Values read from screens and passed on: `ORDER`, `QUOTE`, `REQUEST`, the total, the advance and the balance, the expected return date, the sample's lab code and name.

## Connection findings

1. **The balance holds back no step of the test request.** The Send quotation dialog offers Khi thu mẫu and Trước khi trả kết quả, the default for every order; with the advance verified, confirming the order makes its test request Paid (`_collection_hold`, `sol_commerce/models/orders/sale_order_confirmation.py`).
2. **Head of Sales threshold approval is not built.** Neither sending nor confirming asked for an approval; the main-path approval and WF-01-E3 cannot be walked.
3. **The test request cannot be found by order number.** In Test Requests, the search suggestion Order Number finds nothing for `ORDER`. Sample delivery staff find the request by the customer, or by `REQUEST`, which only Sales sees on the order form: the portal order page does not show it.
4. **PayOS is replaced by WF-01-A4.** The journey records the transfer by Sales and confirms it from the accountant's task queue, all by clicks. The portal offers a second click-only way that is not walked: the quotation page's Bank transfer button opens "Report bank transfer". That page also shows a PayOS button on a request whose collection method is Bank transfer, while Sales's Record payment dialog says PayOS or the company bank account is not configured.
5. **Cypress RPC shortcuts that the journey replaces by screens:** the expected sample pickup is typed in the form; the accountant reaches the payment through the task queue row, which opens the payment; the customer's cart needs no clearing. The customer's bank transfer itself is made at the customer's bank and is marked outside the browser (step 11); WF-01-A4 has Sales record it.
6. **The default Lead way has no subcontracted twin.** On Bottled water the cart offers `Lead (Pb) – TCVN 7602:2007`, the default, and `Lead (Pb) – ISO 17294-2:2016`; only the second has subcontracted ways. The customer chooses it in step 2.
7. **The sample is named after its sample type in the salesperson's language.** The customer cannot name the sample on the portal. After the quotation is sent, the customer's English pages head the expected samples "Nước đóng chai – Bottled water", and the test request lists the sample with Customer's Sample Name `Nước đóng chai`, so WF-02 finds no tagged name.
8. **E-mail.** Sending the quotation attaches `ORDER-R1.pdf` and needs no waiting in the journey.
9. **The customer cannot accept a quotation that asks for an advance.** The portal quotation has a Decline form and the payment request, no Accept button. The quotation turns Accepted, with its acceptance time, when the accountant confirms the advance.
10. **The customer sees an expected return date before Sales has agreed the pickup day.** Right after submitting, the order page shows an expected return date counted from today (2 December in the run); it moves when Sales types the pickup day (3 December for a pickup on 30 November) and then stays the same through sending, payment and confirmation, on the order, the portal and the test request.
11. **The Test Requests search looks in Code first.** Typing the customer's name and pressing Enter finds nothing; staff must pick the suggestion Customer.
12. **Texts in the writer's language.** The accountant's English queue names the task "Xác nhận thanh toán: …", as Sales's recording wrote it; Sales's Vietnamese order shows the messages "Order S##### was submitted." and "… was claimed by the laboratory." in English.
